Chamber of Commerce Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

Lagos Chamber of Commerce

Domain Concept: lagoschamber.org

2. Site Purpose

The Lagos Chamber of Commerce website will serve as a digital resource hub and networking gateway for local businesses, entrepreneurs, and community leaders in the vibrant Lagos metropolitan area. The platform facilitates business directory searches, promotes local economic development, coordinates community events, and fosters partnerships between burgeoning local enterprises and established corporations.

3. Target Market & User Personas

Target Market: Small to medium business owners, startup founders, corporate executives, and local residents in Lagos and surrounding regional economic corridors.


Maria - Small Business Owner

32-year-old boutique owner looking to gain visibility, network with other suppliers, and access chamber marketing opportunities to scale her retail presence.

Diego - Corporate Director

45-year-old logistics director seeking strategic community partnerships, corporate sponsorship packages, and high-impact regional economic forums.

Amina - New Resident & Professional

28-year-old tech professional recently relocated to Lagos, searching for vetted local service providers, co-working networks, and chamber-hosted civic events.

4. Scenarios & User Stories

  • A local artisan searches the business directory by category to identify potential wholesale partners and verify contact info.
  • An expanding technology startup explores membership tiers (Non-profit, Silver, Gold) and submits an online application form with company details.
  • A visitor accesses the Discover page to review weather forecasts, demographic statistics, locale history, and upcoming networking breakfasts.

5. SEO Strategy

  • Target local business search queries: "Lagos Chamber of Commerce", "Lagos business directory", "join chamber Lagos".
  • Implement descriptive meta titles and meta descriptions between 50-160 characters on every page.
  • Utilize Open Graph tags and schema.org Organization structured data.
  • Ensure responsive mobile speed and optimal Core Web Vitals performance.

6. Design Brief

  • Primary Color: #0f2a4a (Deep Navy)
  • Secondary/Accent Color: #0284c7 (Sky Blue) & #16a34a (Emerald Green)
  • Background Color: #f8fafc (Light Slate) & #ffffff (Pure White)
  • Text Color: #1e293b (Dark Slate)
  • Font Family: 'Montserrat', system-ui, sans-serif

7. Site Map & Page Architecture

The website is organized into four core pages:

  • Home: Community highlights, weather forecast, call to action, and business spotlight cards.
  • Discover: Regional history, demographics, area gallery/montage, and local events.
  • Directory: Interactive business directory supporting Grid view and List view toggles.
  • Join: Membership tiers (Non-profit, Silver, Gold), benefits comparison, and application form.
